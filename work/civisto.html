2026 • Civisto new

Invasive species reporting for the Civisto community app

New featureInvasive speciesPWACapacitor

The situation

Civisto is a Progressive Web App that lets citizens report local issues — electricity, garbage, safety, traffic and graffiti — and track them to resolution. What it couldn't capture was invasive species: knotweed pushing through a verge, signal crayfish in a stream, sightings that landowners and authorities need to act on early.

What we did

As Civisto's CTO-as-a-service partner we added an Invasive species category to the report flow — a new category chip alongside the existing ones, with photo capture, automatic location tagging and routing to the right authority. The Civisto codebase is one PWA shipped to web, Android and iOS through Capacitor, so the feature landed on every platform from a single change.

What changed

Communities can now log invasive species sightings the moment they spot them, alongside potholes and graffiti, and the report reaches the people who can act. Civisto keeps its momentum: planned, built and maintained by Lido Development, the holding company behind the product.
