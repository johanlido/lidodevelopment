2024 • Cobalt Bank

Splitting a nine-year monolith into four owned services

websitebrandingSEOfullstack platform

The situation

Nine years of a single Rails app. A weekend to ship. Two teams queued on the same deploy. The board wanted payments spun out as its own product, and the platform team could not say when that would be possible.

What we did

Mapped the bounded contexts that already existed in the codebase, then extracted four services with their own data stores. A strangler facade sat in front of the old app so the website did not notice. Each service got an owner and an on-call rota before the next one left the monolith.

What changed

Four services live. Payments ships on its own cadence. The original app is smaller, still serving the leftover surface, and has a retirement date. Illustrative outcome — replace before launch.
