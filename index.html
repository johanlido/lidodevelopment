How it works

No decks that outlive the problem. Three stages, one goal: make the change stick to how your team actually works.

  1. Week 1–2
    01

  2. With your team
    02

Questions

Before you ask

How is this different from an agency?

An agency sells you delivery capacity against a spec you wrote. We're accountable for whether the spec was right — the decisions, the hiring, the architecture and the number your board sees. When capacity is genuinely the gap, we'll tell you and help you buy it well.

What if we already have engineers?

Most of our clients do. The gap is usually leadership, not hands. We work with the team you have, make the calls that were stuck, and coach the lead who eventually replaces us.

How quickly can you start?

Diagnostics usually start within two weeks. We take a maximum of five concurrent clients so a principal is genuinely available, which means availability is limited and honest.

Do you write code?

Yes — where it unblocks the team or sets a standard worth copying. We don't pretend to be a full delivery team on a Fractional CTO engagement; that's what Embedded is for.

What happens at the end?

A permanent technical lead is hired and coached, the systems are documented, and we drop to a monthly advisory cadence. If we've done the job properly, you stop paying us.
